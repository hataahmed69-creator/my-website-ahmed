Probability Insight
Each coin toss is an independent event with probability:
P(Heads) = P(Tails) = ½
Toss the coin many times to see the Law of Large Numbers in action — the observed frequency will converge toward the theoretical probability of 0.5.
Expected value per toss:
E[X] = 1 × ½ + 0 × ½ = 0.5

Variance: σ² = p(1−p) = 0.25
